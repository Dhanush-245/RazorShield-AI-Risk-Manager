# Risk policy

Fusion scores are presented as LOW 0–30, MEDIUM 31–70, and HIGH 71–100. Low recommends allow; medium recommends additional verification/review; high recommends manual review. These bands are an operational demo policy layered over measured model output and require merchant-specific cost calibration before production.

Rules are transparent evidence inputs rather than final verdicts. Return risk is not fraud. Anomalies are not confirmed fraud. Missing chargeback evidence is marked unavailable. No claimed saving is recorded without a real outcome and attributable action. Reviewer approve/reject/escalate/request-evidence decisions are durable and auditable.
