# RAZORSHIELD AI — ADVANCED AI RISK MANAGER

## Razorpay Buildathon Track 02 — Master Project Prompt

Act as a senior AI/ML architect, fraud-risk engineer, data scientist, GenAI engineer, agentic-AI engineer, backend engineer, MLOps engineer, cybersecurity engineer, and hackathon mentor.

I am building an advanced project called **RazorShield AI** for the **Razorpay Buildathon — Track 02: AI Risk Manager**.

The official track objective is:

> **Stop the merchant losing money to fraud, returns and chargebacks.**

The official directions include:

* Chargeback evidence responder
* Return-risk scorer
* Fraud-spike detector
* Abuse-ring sentinel

The track requires a **working detector, verifier, or auto-responder for one class of loss, with measured precision and recall on a held-out test set.**

The project must be **strictly defense-only**. Never implement functionality that enables fraud, payment abuse, evasion, exploitation, attack automation, or bypassing financial-risk controls.

---

# 1. PROJECT VISION

Build RazorShield AI as a **production-oriented, explainable, evidence-grounded AI Risk Manager** for merchants.

The system should go beyond a simple fraud classifier.

Its core workflow should be:

**DETECT → INVESTIGATE → EXPLAIN → SCORE → RECOMMEND → HUMAN APPROVAL → ACT → AUDIT**

The primary measurable objective will be:

## Fraud-related loss prevention

The **Fraud-Spike Detector** will be the primary benchmarked component.

The other official Track 02 directions will become supporting intelligence:

* Abuse-Ring Sentinel → graph-based risk intelligence
* Return-Risk Scorer → secondary merchant-loss prediction
* Chargeback Evidence Responder → evidence-grounded GenAI workflow

All components must operate as **one integrated risk-management system**, not four disconnected projects.
